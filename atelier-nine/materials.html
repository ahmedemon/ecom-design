Materials

Materials that age well

We choose fibres and leathers for how they look after three years, not three weeks.

Full-grain leather

Used for our sneakers, boots and belts. Full-grain keeps the strongest part of the hide, so it develops a patina rather than cracking.

Sourced from Italian and Portuguese tanneries; chrome-free tanning on our Service Boot.

Extra-fine merino

Fine fibres (under 19.5 microns) that feel soft against the skin and regulate temperature naturally.

Spun in Italy from certified farms in Patagonia; knitted in northern Portugal.

Organic cotton

Used for tees, oxfords, canvas and twills. Grown without synthetic pesticides and garment-dyed in small batches.

GOTS-certified mills in Portugal and Turkey.

Selvedge denim

14oz Japanese denim woven on shuttle looms. The self-finished edge means no fraying, and fades beautifully.

Woven in Okayama, cut and sewn in Portugal.

Waxed cotton

A dense cotton shell treated with a natural wax. Water-resistant and re-waxable for decades of use.

Lancashire, UK.

Recycled synthetics

Used sparingly where performance matters — ripstop uppers and webbing on trail pieces.

Recycled polyester from post-consumer bottles; bluesign®-approved fabrics.
