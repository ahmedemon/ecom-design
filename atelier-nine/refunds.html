Refund policy

Last updated November 7, 2026. This is a template for a demonstration store — have it reviewed before using it for a real business.

60-day returns

You can return unworn items with tags attached within 60 days of delivery for a full refund to your original payment method.

Free exchanges

Need a different size or colour? Exchanges are always free — contact us and we will send the replacement.

How to return

  • Email hello@ateliernine.com with your order number.
  • We will email a prepaid label (US orders).
  • Refunds are issued within 5 working days of receiving the parcel.

Faulty items

If something arrives faulty, we will repair, replace or refund it — at any time.
