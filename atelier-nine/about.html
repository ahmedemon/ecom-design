Our story

Fewer, better things.

Atelier Nine started as a nine-piece wardrobe experiment and grew into a small studio making footwear and everyday clothing.

Built to be worn, not replaced

We make a small range in small runs. Every piece is designed to be worn for years — and repaired when it needs it — rather than replaced each season.

We work with a handful of family-run mills and workshops in Portugal, Japan, the UK and the US, visit each one, and publish who they are.

We re-order only what sells, which keeps waste low and means occasionally selling out. We think that is a fair trade.

What we believe

Materials first

Full-grain leather, extra-fine merino, organic cotton. Good materials age well; bad ones just age.

Transparent making

Every product page lists where it was made, and our Factories page names the people behind it.

Repair, don't replace

Welted boots can be resoled; knitwear can be mended. We will point you to someone who can do it.
